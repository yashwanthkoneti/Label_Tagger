Why the Embedding/Retriever Approach is More "Generic"Decouples Knowledge Retrieval from Text Generation:In a banking risk ecosystem, taxonomies, controls, and policies are static references, while issues are dynamic inputs. An embedding model maps everything into a shared geometric space. This allows you to scale to future domains (e.g., Fraud, AML, Credit Risk) simply by swapping or adding vector indices, without altering model logic or prompt engineering.Deterministic and Auditable:Regulators and internal risk teams require explainability. Finding a risk taxonomy via vector similarity ($\text{cosine similarity}$ or $\text{K-NN}$) allows you to mathematically prove why Issue X was mapped to Control Y (by pointing directly to the nearest neighbor vector distance), whereas causal LLMs are prone to hallucinating structural reasoning paths.Compute and Cost Efficiency:Running batch inference across thousands of bank issues using an embedding model requires a fraction of the compute, memory, and latency compared to spinning up a 7B–24B parameter causal LLM endpoint.
